HOW TO READ THIS

Observations, warnings, forecasts and projections are different things

Earthquakes: recent M2.5+ USGS events drive current severity. The AI layer may project persistence of current activity, but does not predict an exact future earthquake.

Tsunamis: NOAA warning-center messages affect the live score only while fresh. Projection describes persistence of current message state, not the probability of a new tsunami.

Volcanoes: latest USGS observatory notice per volcano drives current severity. Longer persistence reflects the slower-changing nature of alert-state evidence.

Tornadoes: active NWS warnings/watches drive current EMHSI. SPC Day 1 probabilities are official forecast evidence and can support the experimental future-signal projection while valid.

Coverage and source confidence describe authoritative-feed availability, not disaster probability. Projection confidence describes model support and horizon uncertainty, not certainty that an event will occur. For life-safety decisions, follow official alerts and local emergency management.
