HOW TO READ THIS

Monitoring + official warnings + transparent scoring

Earthquakes: recent M2.5+ events across the contiguous U.S., Alaska and Hawaii, with severity driven mainly by the largest recent magnitude.

Tsunamis: NOAA/NWS warning-center messages. Warning, advisory and watch levels affect the live score only while fresh inside the 24-hour safety window.

Volcanoes: the latest USGS observatory notice for each volcano, never the historical maximum alert from older notices.

Tornadoes: active NWS Tornado Warnings and Watches. Later versions add SPC probabilistic outlook layers.

Coverage and source confidence describe authoritative-feed availability, not the probability that a disaster will occur. For life-safety decisions, follow USGS, NOAA/NWS, local emergency management and evacuation instructions.
